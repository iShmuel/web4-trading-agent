Statistical Arbitrage: Zero-Beta Pairs Trading

Market-neutral spread mean reversion with mathematical proof.

The Cointegration Math

Computes rolling hedge ratio: β = Cov(A, B) / Var(B)

Calculates rolling spread: Spread = Price_A - (β × Price_B)

Normalizes to Z-Score: Z = (Spread - Mean) / StdDev

Execution Rules

• |Z| ≥ 2.0: Spread stretched → Long cheap asset, Short rich asset.

• |Z| ≤ 0.5: Mean reversion target hit → Close with zero market risk.

• Market Beta = 0.0: Profits regardless of market direction.
